Information we use

  • Apple or Google sign-in: account identifier, name, email address (including an Apple private relay address), and profile image if provided. Your sign-in provider handles your password; the app does not receive it.
  • Your content: projects, arrangement data, imported and recorded audio, samples, reusable presets, and related metadata. AI chats and editing history can be stored locally as part of app use; project records may contain related instructions or text.
  • Preferences: Gemini API key, model and AI settings, custom instructions, theme and keyboard shortcuts. The key is saved in your browser and also in your private Firebase preferences when signed in. It is a credential: restrict it in Google Cloud and avoid shared devices.
  • Agreement records: Terms and Privacy Policy versions, your adult eligibility confirmation, and an account-linked server timestamp when you continue through Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Technical information: hosting and infrastructure providers process IP addresses, browser and device information, requests, timestamps, authentication and security signals, and operational logs to deliver and protect the service.
  • Correspondence: information you choose to include in support, privacy, or legal requests.

Local storage and cloud sync

The editor stores work and preferences using browser storage, including IndexedDB and localStorage. A service worker caches the app for offline use. Without sign-in, your projects remain on that browser unless you export them, share them, or use optional AI. Signing in synchronizes eligible local projects, audio, library items, and preferences with your private Firebase account. Signing out does not clear local projects, settings, or an API key on a shared device.

These storage technologies support requested features, authentication, preferences, offline use, and security. We do not include advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers. Clearing browser data removes local copies and preferences; browser storage is not a guaranteed backup.

Optional AI, recordings, and online sounds

Using the agent sends prompts, relevant project state, conversation context, and custom instructions to Google Gemini. Attached audio, voice messages, references, sample auditions, and mixes the agent is permitted to listen to are also sent. Microphone access requires your browser permission and an action to record. The "Let the agent listen" setting controls automatic audio auditions during an AI task; you can turn it off in Settings. Review the AI data notice before enabling requests.

Google states that unpaid Gemini inputs and outputs can be used to improve its products and may be reviewed by people. Paid services have different processing terms. Do not send personal, sensitive, confidential, or third-party material you lack permission to submit. Google's AI terms and Privacy Policy explain its practices. The app cannot delete copies already processed by Google; exercise provider rights with Google as applicable.

Online sound searches and downloads connect to Wikimedia Commons and audio asset hosts. Those providers receive your network information and search or file requests. Imported sounds retain their source licenses and attribution requirements.

Retention and security

You can delete your account in the app account settings after verifying ownership. Apple authorization is revoked as part of deleting an Apple-linked account. A record containing only the deleted Firebase UID and deletion time is retained for security, to prevent old authenticated devices from restoring deleted data. Native session tokens are cached in the iOS Keychain and cleared on sign-out or deletion. Cloud account data is retained while needed to provide your account and the features you use, until you delete applicable items or request deletion, subject to legal, security, and dispute requirements. Agreement records and necessary security records may remain where needed to document compliance or resolve claims. Provider logs and backups follow provider retention schedules; removal from those systems may not be immediate. Local copies remain until you clear them or use available deletion controls. We use encrypted network transport, Apple or Google authentication, and account-scoped database rules. No storage or transmission system is completely secure.
